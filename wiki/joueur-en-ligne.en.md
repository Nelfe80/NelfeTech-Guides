# Player online / at home

The NelfeTech account links your venue scores, your Live Contest participations and - down the road - rankings and rating.

## Create your account

On the platform's account area: enter your **email**, open the **login link** you receive (valid 30 minutes). No password to remember.

- **Twitch** can be linked to the account ("Continue with Twitch") - handy if you join Live Contests, never mandatory.
- **RetroAchievements** will be linkable as a verified link (preparing home rankings).

## Your venue scores

There is nothing to claim. It is **the same account** in the venue and online: the sign-in link you open the first time, standing at a cabinet, opens that very account. Your scores ("verified venue" provenance) reach your profile on their own, visit after visit and venue after venue.

!!! note "An anonymous badge to attach"
    A venue can hand an **anonymous badge** to anyone who would rather not give an email. Those scores live without an account at first: the venue gives you a code, and entering it from your account brings them in. It is the only case where a claim still exists.

## Rankings

Public rankings show the best marks per player and per game, with their provenance. Venue scores are signed by the venue's hub - a third party can verify their origin. The **rating** (verified arcade and home, side by side) will arrive after its calibration season.

## Notifications

Turn on **notifications** from your account (Config tab): you're alerted - in the **browser/phone AND by email** - when a contest opens, when a tournament is being prepared at a venue you follow, or when someone **beats your record**. Add the account page to your home screen to receive them like an app.

## Your data

Your account is yours. From the **Config** tab of your account:

- **📦 Export my data** downloads, as JSON, everything the platform knows about you: account, linked identities, attached scores, follows, entries.
- **🗑️ Delete my account** permanently erases the account and everything attached to it (identities, follows, notifications). Your venue scores remain, but **become anonymous again**.

Nothing is kept longer than needed: expired sign-in links and in-transit requests are purged automatically.

## At home

Streamers' Live Contests are played from home: see the [streamer guide](streamer.md) for the viewer journey (one !command in chat, the game launches at home, your runs count live).
